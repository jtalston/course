The user finished a step (or got stuck) and needs the *one* next action. Context they gave: **$ARGUMENTS**

Decision fatigue is the enemy. Do NOT present options or re-plan the whole task.

1. If they said they finished something, acknowledge it in a few words — momentum matters more than praise.
2. Give **exactly one** next action, concrete and under ~10 minutes.
3. If they said they're stuck, make the next step even smaller — split it in half, or make it "just open the file and read it." Lowering the bar is the fix, not a pep talk.
4. If the overall task now looks finished, say so plainly and suggest they stop and mark the block complete.

One action. One line for why it's next. That's it.
