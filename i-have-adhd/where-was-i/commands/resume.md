description: Pick up exactly where you left off — no re-reading, no cold start.
---

The user is back after a break or interruption and needs to get their head back into the work.

1. Read `.adhd/breadcrumb.md`. If it doesn't exist, say so kindly and offer to reconstruct from the recent conversation instead.
2. **Re-orient them in three lines, no more:**
   - Where they were (the task)
   - The exact next step they'd lined up
   - The one don't-forget detail
3. **Don't re-explain the whole history** — that's a re-entry tax, not help. Just enough to feel oriented.
4. Optionally glance at git status or recently changed files to confirm the breadcrumb still matches reality, and flag it if something changed while they were away.
5. End by pointing at the next step and asking if they're ready to go — or if they want to hand it to `/focus:next` to shrink it further.

The feeling you're aiming for: "oh right, I've got this" — not "ugh, where do I even start."
